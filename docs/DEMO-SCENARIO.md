## Evidence and institutional claims

All candidate records, evidence, case events, communications, fees, policies and institution interactions are synthetic unless explicitly linked to a verified public source. A fictional adviser marking an item "checked" does not mean an institution accepted it.

Do not present invented policy quotations, official fee amounts, handbook clauses, statutory interpretations, university-issued letters, or API integrations as verified facts. Where there is no source, label the item "Illustrative demo rule", "Sample evidence", or "Awaiting source verification".

This prototype does not claim partnership, endorsement, official policy validation, or live integration with Taylor's University, EMGS, MQA, or any other institution.

## Interaction honesty

This site has no production backend. Buttons may demonstrate local UI states, previews or simulated confirmations. They must not claim a real file was transmitted, a university was contacted, an appointment was booked, or an application was submitted. Use explicit "Demo action only — nothing is transmitted" language.

## Privacy

Use synthetic and masked information only. Never publish real passport numbers, bank statements, private email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses or student documents.
